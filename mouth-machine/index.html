Timing & mic

If your hits keep landing late, calibrate. AirPods and other Bluetooth headphones add 150–300 ms of delay and switch your phone to a lower-quality mic; wired headphones give the best timing. Imported recordings line up on their first sound, so the delay setting only affects live recording.
