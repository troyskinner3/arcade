Common questions

Noodle doesn’t hear me, or notes land late. Allow the microphone in Settings → Noodle. For late notes: More → Mic and timing → Fix late notes.

I bought Noodle Pro on another phone. More → Noodle Pro → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

What does the sparkle mean? It marks Noodle Pro. You can try any Pro sound or effect on your song first; it goes back when you close the menu unless you have Pro.

How do I stop sharing a song link? Open the song → Share → Stop sharing. The link stops working right away.

Someone sent me a song that shouldn’t be there. Open it → Share → Report, or tap “Report this song” on its web page. A song reported by several people is taken down.

How do I keep my songs safe? More → Back up all songs saves one file with every song and its recordings. More → Open a song file brings them back.

How do I delete what I shared? More → Share my takes → turn it off and choose to delete what was sent. For anything else, email us.
