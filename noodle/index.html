How it works

  1. Tap + Add part and pick an instrument: drums, a melody instrument, or vocals.
  2. Tap Record on it. After four clicks, beatbox, sing, hum or whistle along with the click.
  3. Tap a part to open it, fix notes or change its sound. Export when you’re done.

The parts below are an example groove: press play to hear them. Your first recording replaces them.

Share your song

For Messages, CapCut, TikTok, Reels or anywhere else. Audio is a WAV file; video is a vertical clip with your parts moving to the beat.

Producer pack (.zip)

For Logic, Ableton, FL Studio or GarageBand: every part as a 24-bit WAV starting at bar 1, your raw vocal, MIDI for each part with tempo and key, and step-by-step import notes.

If your hits keep landing late, calibrate. AirPods and other Bluetooth headphones add 150–300 ms of delay and switch your phone to a lower-quality mic; wired headphones give the best timing. Imported recordings line up on their first sound, so the delay setting only affects live recording.

Record

First time recording here? Calibrate once (about 10 seconds of clapping) so your hits land on the right steps. Bluetooth headphones like AirPods add a big delay, so calibrate with the headphones you’ll use.
