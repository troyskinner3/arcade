Privacy policy

Effective date at launch

Draft for review. Not yet in effect and not legal advice. Items marked like this are decisions or details still to fill in. Have a lawyer or a privacy-policy service review it before launch.

The short version

  • Your recordings and songs stay on your device unless you export them or choose to share takes.
  • Noodle has no accounts, no ads and no tracking.
  • Sharing takes to help improve Noodle is off unless you turn it on, is for people 13 and older, and can be undone: you can stop and delete what you shared.

What stays on your device

  • Everything you record, the songs you make, and your settings are stored on your device.
  • Noodle turns your voice into notes on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere for that.
  • Exports (audio, MIDI, video) are files you save or send yourself. Where they go is up to you.

The microphone

Noodle uses the microphone only while you are recording a take. It is released when the take ends.

If you turn on “Share my takes”

Sharing is optional and off by default. If you turn it on, each take you record is sent to us:

  • the recording of that take (your voice, as a short audio clip);
  • the notes Noodle heard, and the notes after your edits;
  • the song's settings for that take (tempo, key, instrument, timing settings);
  • how noisy the surroundings were, and whether you used the app or the website;
  • when it was recorded;
  • a random ID made on your device, so your takes can be kept together and deleted when you ask.

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location or advertising ID, and we do not store your IP address with your takes.

How shared takes are used

  • To make Noodle better at hearing what people mean: measuring mistakes, and training and testing the software that turns voices into notes.
  • People working on Noodle may listen to shared recordings for that purpose.
  • We do not use recordings to identify anyone and do not make voiceprints.
  • We do not sell shared takes, and we do not share them with anyone except the hosting provider that stores them for us (Cloudflare).

Your choices

  • Stop sharing at any time in Noodle's settings. New takes are then no longer sent.
  • Delete what you shared from the same place. This removes every take shared from that device from our storage.
  • The random ID lives only on your device. If you delete the app or clear its data first, we can no longer tell which takes were yours, so delete your shared takes before removing the app.
  • We keep shared takes until you delete them, or for up to 3 years (decide), whichever comes first.

Children

Sharing takes is only for people aged 13 and older, and Noodle asks before turning it on. We do not knowingly collect recordings from children under 13. If you believe a child has shared takes, contact us and we will delete them.

The website

The web version of Noodle is hosted by GitHub, which, like any web host, receives your IP address when your browser loads the page. Noodle uses no cookies. It uses your browser's storage only to keep your songs and settings on your device.

Your rights

Sharing is based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time as described above. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to ask for a copy of your data or to have it corrected or deleted. Because shared takes are tied only to a random ID on your device, the in-app controls are the reliable way to use these rights.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects shared takes, Noodle will ask you again before sharing continues.
