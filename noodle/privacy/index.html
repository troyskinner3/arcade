Privacy policy

Effective 22 November 2026

The short version

  • Your recordings and songs stay on your device unless you export them or choose to share takes.
  • Noodle has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Email news is optional.
  • Sharing takes to help improve Noodle is off unless you turn it on, is for people 13 and older, and can be undone: you can stop and delete what you shared.

What stays on your device

  • Everything you record, the songs you make, and your settings are stored on your device.
  • Noodle turns your voice into notes on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere for that.
  • Exports (audio, MIDI, video) and song files (a backup of your songs and their recordings) are files you save or send yourself. Where they go is up to you.
  • “Your voice”, an instrument made from your own singing, is stored inside the song on your device, like the rest of the song.

The microphone

Noodle uses the microphone only while you are recording a take. It is released when the take ends.

If you turn on “Share my takes”

Sharing is optional and off by default. If you turn it on, each take you record is sent to us:

  • the recording of that take (your voice, as a short audio clip);
  • the notes Noodle heard, the notes after your edits, and which notes you changed;
  • whether you kept, discarded or exported the take;
  • the song's name and its settings for that take (tempo, key, instrument, timing settings);
  • how noisy the surroundings were, and whether you used the app or the website;
  • when it was recorded;
  • a random ID made on your device, so your takes can be kept together and deleted when you ask.

If you record without a connection, shared takes wait on your device and are sent when you are back online. Turning sharing off empties that waiting list.

With your takes we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location or advertising ID, and we do not store your IP address.

How shared takes are used

  • To make Noodle better at hearing what people mean: measuring mistakes, and training and testing the software that turns voices into notes.
  • People working on Noodle may listen to shared recordings for that purpose.
  • We do not use recordings to identify anyone and do not make voiceprints.
  • We do not sell shared takes, and we do not share them with anyone except the hosting provider that stores them for us (Cloudflare).

Your choices

  • Stop sharing at any time in Noodle's settings. New takes are then no longer sent.
  • Delete what you shared from the same place. This removes every take shared from that device from our storage.
  • The random ID lives only on your device. If you delete the app or clear its data first, we can no longer tell which takes were yours, so delete your shared takes before removing the app.
  • We keep shared takes until you delete them, or for up to 1 year after you recorded them, whichever comes first. After that they are removed automatically.

Crash reports

  • When something goes wrong in Noodle, it sends us a short report so we can fix it: the error message and where in the code it happened, the version of Noodle, whether it was the app or the website, and which screen you were on.
  • Reports contain no recordings, no songs or song names, and no ID. They are not linked to you or to your shared takes. We keep them for 90 days.
  • Crash reports are on unless you turn them off: More → Send crash reports.

Usage stats

  • Noodle sends us counts of how it gets used, so we can see where people get stuck and what to improve: for example that a part was recorded (and its type, like Drums or Bass), that a song was exported or shared (and what kind, like audio or a before-and-after video, and its length in seconds), which sounds a finished song uses, that the Noodle Pro page was opened or a Pro sound was tried, that Send to computer was used (how many notes were sent, the delay on the phone, and whether a computer was connected by cable or another way, never its name), and how many days it has been since Noodle was first opened on this device.
  • Each count also says the version of Noodle, whether it was the app or the website, whether you have Noodle Pro, and a random ID made on your device only for usage stats. The ID lets us see how one device moves through Noodle over time (for example, whether people who record a first part go on to finish a song). It is a different ID from the one used for shared takes, song links and invites, and we never connect it to your email address, your purchases or anything else about you.
  • Usage stats contain no recordings, no songs or song names, and no IP address. They are stored by our hosting provider (Cloudflare) and kept for 3 months.
  • Usage stats are on unless you turn them off: More → Send usage stats. Turning them off deletes the ID from your device; if you turn them on again, a new one is made.

If you share a song by link

  • Share → Link to remix uploads that one song to our server: its parts, settings and name, and its recordings (vocals and other audio parts), plus the first 30 seconds as audio for the listen page.
  • Anyone with the link can listen to it and copy it into their own Noodle to remix it. Don't share a link to anything you want kept private.
  • With it we keep a random ID made on your phone, stored only in scrambled (hashed) form, so that only your phone can update or delete the link. It is not linked to your name, email or shared takes.
  • You can delete a link any time: Share → Stop sharing. Links are deleted on their own after 2 years. Anyone can report a song (in Noodle: Share → Report; or on its page), choosing a reason; we keep the reasons chosen, not who reported. A reported song is hidden until we have looked at it.

Invites

  • More → Invite friends asks our server for your invite code. To count your friends, we keep your code, a scrambled (hashed) form of a random ID made on your phone, and the hashed IDs of friends' phones that joined with your code. Nothing about who those friends are.
  • When you use a friend's code in the iPhone app, your phone sends it with its own hashed ID once you have made a song, plus a one-time token from Apple's DeviceCheck. We pass that token to Apple only to confirm it comes from a real iPhone and to mark that this iPhone has used an invite (Apple keeps that one mark for us; it tells us nothing about you, and we don't store the token). If you earn Noodle Pro, we keep which offer code you were given, so it can be shown to you again.

If you sign up for email news

  • Noodle may offer to email you news about Noodle and other things we are building. This is optional and separate from sharing takes.
  • If you sign up, we keep your email address, the date, and the wording you agreed to. It is not linked to your takes or to anything else you do in Noodle.
  • Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can ask us to remove your address at any time.

Children

Sharing takes is only for people aged 13 and older, and Noodle asks before turning it on. We do not knowingly collect recordings from children under 13. If you believe a child has shared takes, contact us and we will delete them.

The website

The web version of Noodle is hosted by GitHub, which, like any web host, receives your IP address when your browser loads the page. Noodle uses no cookies. It uses your browser's storage only to keep your songs and settings on your device.

Your rights

Sharing is based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time as described above. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to ask for a copy of your data or to have it corrected or deleted. Because shared takes are tied only to a random ID on your device, the in-app controls are the reliable way to use these rights.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects shared takes, Noodle will ask you again before sharing continues.
