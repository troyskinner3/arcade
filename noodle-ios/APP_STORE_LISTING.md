### Description (4000)
Get the song out of your head.

Hum, beatbox or sing each part, and Noodle turns it into drums, bass, chords, melodies and vocals on a beat grid. No instruments, no music theory, no account.

HOW IT WORKS
• Beatbox a groove ("b", "ch", "ts") and it becomes a drum kit
• Hum a bassline or melody and it becomes notes you can play on piano, strings, guitar, synths and more
• Sing your real vocals: Noodle tunes them to the song's key and polishes them like a record
• Tap along while you hum to catch every repeated note
• Fix anything with a tap: move, split or nudge notes, select several at once, undo everything

MAKE IT SOUND LIKE A SONG
• Chords from your melody: hum a tune and Noodle writes chords that fit it
• Drum fills and crashes every 4 or 8 bars
• Filter sweeps you draw yourself: shape low-pass and high-pass curves over your song
• Name your verse and chorus, loop them, duplicate them
• Change the key and the whole song moves with you
• Styles: Pop, Hip hop, EDM, Rock, Lo-fi
• Recorded piano, strings, rock drums, e-piano, bass and guitar, free

SHARE IT
• Export audio or a vertical video for TikTok, Reels and YouTube Shorts
• Send a link: friends listen, then open it in Noodle and add their own part

NOODLE PRO: TRY IT ON YOUR SONG FIRST
Every Pro sound and effect can be tried on your own song before you buy. Love it? Pro is a one-time purchase, yours for life.
• More recorded instruments: trumpet, clarinet, horns, flute, marimba, xylophone, glockenspiel
• Vocoder and talkbox vocals, and backing vocals made from your own voice
• 9 more song effects: phaser, washout, echo throw, vinyl brake, bitcrusher and more
• Echo, chorus and drive on any track
• DAW pack: every track as audio and MIDI for Logic, Ableton and GarageBand
• Videos without the Noodle tag
• Invite 3 friends who make a song and get Pro free

Your songs stay on your phone. Noodle has no ads and no account, and it never tracks you.

## Notes for App Review
> Noodle turns humming, beatboxing and singing into music. To try it: open the app, skip or finish the short intro, tap the red record button, pick "Lead", wait for the 4-beat count-in and hum a short tune. No account is needed.
> The microphone is used only to record your takes, which stay on the device unless you choose to share them.
> Pro options carry a small amber sparkle; without Pro they can be tried (heard on your song) but not kept. Purchase: More → Noodle Pro. Restore is in the same sheet.
> Song links (Share → Link to remix) upload one song so friends can open it; the sharer can delete it from the same sheet. Songs from other people can be reported in the app (Share → Report, with a reason) or on their web page; several reports take a song down. Contact: info@tuttii.io (More → Help and contact).
